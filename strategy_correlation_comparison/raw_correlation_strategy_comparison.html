Decision

Best native-horizon beta candidate: ONI → COP relative to XLE over six months, because it has the largest raw magnitude (r=-0.478), the tightest association among the compared families (r²=22.8%) and survives the global energy screen. Its relationship is negative.

Best common-horizon candidate: Nickel return lag 3 → VRT (1m target), r=-0.343. This prevents the six- and twelve-month energy targets from receiving an unfair smoothing advantage.

Recommended beta research order: first estimate and validate COP/XLE on a locked future holdout; second test nickel/VRT on a longer history; third test the one-month PSX/XLE ΔONI relationship. Raw r ranks linear fit, not beta magnitude, economic causality or expected P&L.
