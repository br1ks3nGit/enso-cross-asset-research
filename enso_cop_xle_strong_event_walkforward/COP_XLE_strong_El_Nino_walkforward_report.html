Five signal specifications

  • S1 Strong level: frozen ONI ≥ 1.0.
  • S2 Very strong: ONI ≥ 1.5.
  • S3 Strong and rising: ONI ≥ 1.0 and ΔONI > 0.
  • S4 Rapid strengthening: ONI ≥ 1.0 and ΔONI ≥ 0.3.
  • S5 Strong but cooling: ONI remains ≥ 1.0 but ΔONI < 0. This is a post-strengthening signal, not an ex-post declaration of the exact peak.

Walk-forward mechanics

  1. A centered ONI observation for month m becomes eligible only after month m+2. The trade begins at the next common COP/XLE session's open.
  2. For each rule and holding period, training uses only earlier qualifying observations whose exits occurred before the new entry date.
  3. With at least 3 completed training observations, the sign of the historical mean COP-minus-XLE return sets the position. Negative mean produces long XLE/short COP; positive mean produces long COP/short XLE.
  4. The direction, rule and horizon are frozen for that trade. A strategy does not open a new trade while its preceding trade remains active.

Return, commission and tax formulas

The capital-normalized pair return is 0.5 × long-leg total return − 0.5 × short-leg total return. Each leg receives 50% notional, producing 100% gross exposure and zero initial net market value. Massive split-adjusted prices are supplemented with split-adjusted cash dividends; the short leg owes dividends during the holding period.

Commission is 10 basis points per order notional. Two 50% legs traded at entry and exit produce a 20-basis-point round-trip cost on capital: 2 × 0.10% = 0.20%. Net pre-tax return is gross return − 0.20%.

The base personal-tax illustration assumes a US taxable individual. It nets realized trade profit and loss within each calendar year and charges 37% on a positive annual net short-term result. It assumes no state tax, 3.8% net-investment-income tax, loss carryforward, borrowing cost, short-borrow fee, margin interest or slippage. For simplicity, the XLE buy-and-hold illustration applies a 20% rate to its positive total return, including dividends, at the end of the test. Actual dividend and capital-gain timing will differ. Tax results are scenario estimates, not personal tax advice.

Limitations

  • The supplied ONI series is revised history. The two-month embargo prevents premature use of recent values but does not reconstruct exact NOAA vintages.
  • The test has effectively one completed strong event. Counts of monthly signals overstate the number of independent climate episodes.
  • Choosing the best of 35 rule-and-horizon combinations introduces selection bias. No untouched second strong event exists inside 2020–2026 for final validation.
  • Daily closes and dividends come from Massive. The execution model uses next-session open and horizon-session close, but it does not include bid-ask spreads, locate constraints or market impact.
  • COP/XLE is a relative trade. It is not equivalent to a directional investment in COP or XLE.
