Event study and delayed-signal backtest · 12 September 2026

El Niño peaks and U.S. energy equities

Monthly Massive total-return-adjusted prices show nine delayed-ONI predictive relationships that survive global false-discovery control. They concentrate in ConocoPhillips, refiners and SLB. The narrower ex-post peak study and the implementable peak-confirmation strategy do not produce statistically reliable returns.

Peak definition and information timing

An ONI-defined warm episode requires at least five consecutive monthly centered seasons at or above 0.5°C. The peak is the maximum revised ONI within the run. This is a retrospective event date. The implementable confirmation date is two months after the first centered ONI decline following the peak; trading starts the next month.

Key tendencies

COP underperformed XLE at high ONI

The six-month correlation is -0.478. A +1°C ONI difference corresponds to -5.5% in subsequent six-month log performance versus XLE (global q=0.003). The sign persists after removing COVID-overlapping windows.

Refiners weakened later

The retrospective refiner average is -16.0% versus SPY six months after a peak and -20.6% after 12 months. Only four eligible events exist, and neither result survives multiplicity correction.

PSX showed short-horizon relative strength

Rising ΔONI correlates with PSX outperformance versus XLE over three months: r=0.285, global q=0.039. This differs from PSX’s negative longer-horizon relationship versus SPY.

SLB sensitivity was negative

ΔONI versus next-three-month SLB performance relative to SPY has r=-0.268 and global q=0.044. The post-2015 subsample is weaker, so stability is incomplete.

No broad peak effect

XLE averaged -1.3% versus SPY in the six months after ONI peaks (p=0.848). The broad energy sector did not systematically outperform after peaks.

Monthly frequency is sufficient

ONI changes monthly and is smoothed over three months. Daily equity bars would not create additional independent ENSO signals.

Data and limitations

Massive market data

Monthly qualifying-trade OHLC bars were requested with adjusted=true. Massive’s separate dividend endpoint supplies historical adjustment factors, which were applied to build total-return-adjusted price indexes. September 2026 partial bars are excluded.

Universe and identity controls

The predeclared representative universe covers integrated oil, exploration and production, oilfield services, refining and midstream. MPC is restricted to July 2011 onward, PSX to May 2012 and KMI to February 2011 to avoid earlier ticker histories or predecessor regimes. COP is restricted to June 2012 onward because the April 2012 Phillips 66 spin-off is not a normal price return. The current-company sample has survivorship and selection bias.

ONI vintages

The two-month availability delay is applied to today’s revised ONI history. It prevents direct timing leakage but does not reconstruct historical preliminary vintages or later revisions.

Inference

Forward horizons overlap, so HAC errors are used. Event p-values rely on only a handful of peaks. BH q-values address multiple tests within each displayed family but do not eliminate universe-selection bias or macroeconomic confounding.
